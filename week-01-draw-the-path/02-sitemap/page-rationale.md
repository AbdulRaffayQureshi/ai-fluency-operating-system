## Sitemap (4 Pages)

1. Hero / Landing
2. Work / Case Studies
3. About → "How I Work"
4. Contact / Book Consultation

---

## Page-by-Page Justification

### 1. Hero / Landing
- **Purpose:** States the claim immediately.
- **Content:** Headline = proof statement. CTA = View Case Studies.
- **Earns its place:** Visitor knows within 3 seconds what I prove.

### 2. Work / Case Studies
- **Purpose:** Proves the claim with evidence.
- **Content:** 2-3 projects with metrics, each following a 5-beat proof template (Problem → Architecture → Data contracts → Repro instructions → Outcome).
- **Earns its place:** Without proof, the claim is empty. This is the load-bearing page.

### 3. About → "How I Work"
- **Purpose:** Builds trust *through evidence*, not biography. Repurposed after pressure-test.
- **Content:** Engineering philosophy — testing approach, data contract methodology, CI/CD rigor, how I handle production failure. Functions as more proof, dressed as bio.
- **Earns its place:** A skeptical technical buyer skips a LinkedIn summary. They won't skip a section that validates "reproducible code" and "rigorous data contracts" with concrete process details.

### 4. Contact
- **Purpose:** Enables the one action.
- **Content:** Calendly link to book a project consultation.
- **Earns its place:** This is the conversion point.
